# Contributing Guidelines

Thanks for helping make this list better! This list focuses on **super-resolution of geospatial / Earth-observation data**: optical satellite & aerial imagery, multi-image SR, pansharpening and band sharpening, hyperspectral/thermal fusion, and SAR SR.

## What belongs here

- Peer-reviewed papers, preprints, and theses directly related to geospatial SR.
- Datasets usable for training or benchmarking geospatial SR (real LR-HR pairs preferred).
- Open-source code, models, and tools (research or operational).
- Evaluation protocols, metrics, benchmarks, competitions.
- High-quality tutorials, courses, and survey material.

## What does not belong here

- Generic image SR content with no geospatial relevance (a small set of foundational SISR papers/frameworks is kept on purpose).
- Closed products with no public documentation or evaluation.
- Self-promotion without substance (papers with no code/data and no citations will be judged case by case).

## How to contribute

1. **Fork** the repository and create a branch.
2. Add your entry to the **correct section**, keeping the existing format:
   - Tables: `| Name | Notes | Link |` style, one row per entry.
   - Papers: `Authors, *Title*, Venue, Year. [Link]`.
3. Check that:
   - Links work and point to the canonical source (DOI/arXiv/official repo).
   - No duplicates exist.
   - Descriptions are short, factual, and neutral.
4. Open a **pull request** with a one-line justification per addition.

## Fixing errors

Broken links, wrong attributions, or outdated claims: please open an issue or a PR directly. Corrections are prioritized over additions.
